Built for the room

The phone becomes the host.

No boards, no cards, no downloads for your friends. One phone runs the whole night — and everyone plays.

01

Fair play, guaranteed

Every secret sits behind a pass screen with hold-to-reveal cards — the phone-holder can't peek at roles, answers or votes before they should.

02

Zero-friction rounds

Every game explains itself the first time you open it, saved players rejoin in one tap, and Rematch re-runs the same setup instantly with fresh content.

03

A Hall of Fame that remembers

Wins, records and 12 unlockable achievements persist across sessions — plus shareable winner cards for the group chat.

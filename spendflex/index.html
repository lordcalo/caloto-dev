Our position on privacy

Your data, only yours. On your device, and nowhere else.

There is no account to create, no bank to connect, and no SpendFlex server holding your records — they live on your device, offline-first. When you want a backup, it is a file you own, saved to storage you choose: your Files app, your iCloud Drive, your Google Drive.

  • On-device storage Offline-first records. No server holds your ledger.
  • Face ID / Touch ID lock The app opens for you, and only you.
  • Private backup & restore Your history travels as your own file, nowhere else.
